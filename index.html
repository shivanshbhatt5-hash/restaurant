The Heritage of L'Or Noir

An Odyssey of Black Truffle & Golden Grandeur

"True fine dining is not merely nourishment; it is an unforgettable theater of texture, light, and sensory revelation."

Founded upon the neoclassical traditions of French haute gastronomie, L’OR NOIR was conceived as an ode to the two most precious elements of earth: the mystique of the subterranean black truffle and the radiant purity of gold.

Within our gilded halls and warm champagne marble salon, every dish is an intimate performance. Rare heirloom harvests are transported directly from small biodynamic estates, transformed through time-honored techniques, and plated upon custom porcelain rimmed in liquid gold.
